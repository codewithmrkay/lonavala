A modern trip planning app built with **React.js**, **FireBase**, and **Gemini AI**, empowering travelers with tailored itineraries—from solo adventures to group journeys. Enjoy personalized travel plans featuring budget-friendly hotel recommendations, curated points of interest, and robust secure authentication.

## ✨Features

- 🛫 **Travel for Everyone:** Plan trips perfectly tailored from solo adventures to group journeys.  
- 🎯 **Point of Interest Recommendations:** Discover the best must-visit spots at every destination.  
- 🏨 **Budget-friendly Hotel Picks:** Get curated hotel suggestions that match your budget.  
- 🗺️ **Personalized Itineraries:** Enjoy customized travel plans that suit your style and needs.  
- 🔐 **Secure Access:** Benefit from robust authentication to keep your travel data safe.

- React.js ≥ 18  
- FireBase URI  (get from FireBase Studio)  
- Gemini API Key (get from Google AI Studio)
